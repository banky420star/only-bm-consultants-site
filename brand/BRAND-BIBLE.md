# Only B&M Consultants — Brand Bible v2.0

## Brand core
**Positioning:** Premium brand and marketing design consultancy combining strategy, identity, campaigns and digital experience.

**Primary tagline:** Build the brand. Move the market.

**Purpose:** Turn ambitious businesses into clear, distinctive and commercially valuable brands.

**Principles:** Clarity before decoration. Distinction before noise. Systems before tactics. Prestige through precision.

## Identity logic
- **B:** masculine, grounded, geometric, bold, commercially disciplined.
- **M:** feminine, elegant, refined, expressive, culturally intuitive.
- **&:** the bridge between strategy and creativity.

## Colour system
- Deep Black — #0A0B0A
- Dark Emerald — #0F3D32
- Warm Ivory — #F5F1E8
- Stone — #C8C2B8
- Taupe — #8A8074

## Typography
- Strong sans for structure, UI, labels and commercial statements.
- Refined serif for editorial headlines, quotations and expressive moments.
- Current production fallbacks: Arial/Helvetica + Georgia.

## Verbal identity
Direct, intelligent, confident, concise, commercially literate and culturally aware.

Secondary lines:
- Ideas into impact.
- More than brands.
- Strategy meets creativity.
- Brands meet what’s next.
- A more meaningful tomorrow.

## Visual world
Architecture, materiality, editorial portraiture, sculptural form, controlled foliage, shadow, tactile surfaces, grid lines, thin rules, oversized typography and restrained emerald accents.

## Motion
Slow, deliberate and editorial. Approved interactions include scroll reveals, magnetic buttons, restrained parallax, image drift, animated rules, cursor states, card tilt, progress indicators and case-study hover reveals.

## Services
Brand Strategy · Identity Design · Marketing Systems · Content & Campaigns · Web Experience.
