02 / Identity Logic

The B

Masculine, grounded, geometric and commercially disciplined. It represents structure, decisiveness, scale and confidence.

The M

Feminine, refined, expressive and culturally intuitive. It represents elegance, emotion, taste and movement.

The ampersand is the bridge: the point where strategy meets creativity and commercial logic meets cultural relevance.

Logo usage

Primary lock-up: ONLY B&M / Only B&M Consultants / Brand & Marketing Design Consultancy. Maintain generous clear space. Do not stretch, outline, bevel, rotate, shadow, recolour indiscriminately or place the mark over visually noisy backgrounds.

04 / Typography & Layout

ONLY / STRUCTURE

Primary sans: Helvetica Neue / Arial fallback. Use for identity, navigation, labels, systems and bold commercial statements.

Meaning / Expression

Editorial serif: Georgia fallback. Use for prestige headlines, quotations, contrast moments and expressive copy.

Layout system: editorial grid, generous negative space, thin rules, oversized type, asymmetric composition, tactile imagery and strong hierarchy. Avoid generic startup-card layouts and decorative clutter.

05 / Verbal Identity

Voice

Direct, intelligent, confident, concise, commercially literate and culturally aware.

Use: “We clarify the offer.” “We build the system.” “We measure what moves.”

Avoid

Empty hype, generic “game-changing” language, over-explaining, motivational clichés, excessive jargon and trend-chasing copy.

Primary tagline: Build the brand. Move the market.

Secondary lines: Ideas into impact. / More than brands. / Strategy meets creativity. / Brands meet what’s next. / A more meaningful tomorrow.

06 / Visual World
Imagery

Architecture, materiality, fashion-editorial portraiture, sculptural form, shadow, foliage and refined cultural references.

Photography

Desaturated or controlled colour, high contrast, strong cropping, tactile surfaces and quiet confidence.

Graphic devices

Grid lines, thin rules, circles/orbits, oversized type, restrained emerald accents, labels and indexed sections.

Avoid

Stock-business imagery, rainbow gradients, excessive neon, generic blobs, clip-art iconography and noisy effects.

07 / Motion & Micro-interactions

Motion character

Slow, deliberate, cinematic, minimal and editorial. Motion should signal taste rather than technology for its own sake.

Approved interactions

Scroll reveals, magnetic buttons, image drift, restrained parallax, animated rules, cursor states, card tilt, text masks, progress indicators and case-study hover reveals.

Intro sequence: approximately 5.4 seconds, with ONLY, B, &, M, emerald rule and supporting metadata revealed sequentially before dissolving into the homepage.
